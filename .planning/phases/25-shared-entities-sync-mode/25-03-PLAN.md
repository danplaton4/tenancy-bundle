<objective>
Deliver the two Doctrine event subscribers that are the heart of SHARE-01: the landlord-side `SharedEntitySyncSubscriber` (buffer in `onFlush`, fan out in `postFlush`) and the tenant-side `SharedEntityWriteProtectionListener` (`onFlush` read-only guard). This is new territory — the bundle has no prior Doctrine event subscriber — so both are role-matched to the early-return-guard pattern in `TenantContextOrchestrator` while implementing the Doctrine `EventSubscriber` contract.

Three CRITICAL verified facts drive the implementation (do not deviate):
1. **Buffer in onFlush, NOT postFlush.** ORM 3.6.3 `UnitOfWork::executeInserts/Updates/Deletions()` drains the scheduled-entity arrays before `postFlush` fires — by `postFlush` they are EMPTY. Buffer `#[Shared]` changesets in `onFlush` on the subscriber instance; apply in `postFlush`.
2. **No `merge()`.** ORM 3.0 removed `EntityManagerInterface::merge()`. Upsert via find-or-new + `ClassMetadata::newInstance()` + `getFieldNames()`/`getFieldValue()`/`setFieldValue()`. Copy SCALAR fields only — never iterate `getAssociationNames()` (that is the one-level cascade boundary, DEC-SHARE-02).
3. **Re-entrancy guard.** The subscriber's own `$tenantEm->flush()` triggers the tenant `onFlush`, which would otherwise throw the write-protection exception. The subscriber sets `isSyncInProgress()=true` around its sync writes; the write-protection listener bypasses when that flag is set.

Purpose: synchronous, best-effort, read-only-on-tenant fan-out of landlord master records.
Output: `src/Subscriber/SharedEntitySyncSubscriber.php`, `src/Subscriber/SharedEntityWriteProtectionListener.php` (new `src/Subscriber/` directory).
</objective>
