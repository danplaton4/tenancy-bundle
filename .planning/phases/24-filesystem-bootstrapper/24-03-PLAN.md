<objective>
Ship the per-tenant FilesystemOperator cache plus its context-cleared listener. The cache is consumed by Plan 24-06's `TenantAwareFilesystemDecorator` (per_tenant_adapter mode) and flushed by both Plan 24-07's `FilesystemBootstrapper::clear()` (BootstrapperChain teardown path) and this plan's listener (TenantContextCleared event path) — the same belt-and-suspenders pattern Phase 20 ships for LruTransportCache.
