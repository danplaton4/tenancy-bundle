### Integration warnings (tech debt, not blockers)
- **W-01 (SHARE-01):** `SharedEntitySyncSubscriber` + `SharedEntityWriteProtectionListener` type-hint the concrete `final SharedEntityCopier` rather than `SharedEntityCopierInterface` → cannot inject a mock copier (testability gap). `SharedEntitySyncSubscriber.php:97`.
- **W-02 (SHARE-03):** `SharedEntityChangedMessageHandler` duplicates `switchToTenant()`/`restoreTenantContext()` byte-for-byte from the subscriber (acknowledged "keep in sync"). Future divergence would become a blocker. `SharedEntityChangedMessageHandler.php:47-49,169-207`.
- **W-03 (SHARE-02 ↔ SHARE-01):** resync command switches tenants via `setTenant()+bootstrapperChain->boot()` (fires `TenantBootstrapped` + all bootstrappers); subscriber/handler use `close()+resetManager()` directly (skip them). Intentional perf trade-off but behaviorally asymmetric. `SharedEntityResyncCommand.php:126-127` vs `SharedEntitySyncSubscriber.php:252-262`.
